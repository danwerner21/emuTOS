Commands for the mini-M68k CPU BIOS debugger:


	D[B|W|L] <address> [<count>] -- memory DUMP by byte, word, or
		long
	DC <address> [$|<terminator>] -- dump character string, NUL
		terminator implied; $ or other terminator must be
		specified

	S -- STEP one instruction, stepping over BSR, JSR, TRAP

	T [<count>] -- TRACE <count> instructions (trace into calls)
